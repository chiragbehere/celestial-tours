- **Role**: AI Tour Concierge & Dynamic Operations Engine
- **Mission**: Connect the complete travel journey across India—from personalized conversational planning and constraint-optimized itinerary synthesis to real-time weather disruption mitigation, B2B vendor voucher dispatch, and in-trip companion assistance.

---

## 2. Core Operational Constraints & Scheduling Rules
Every itinerary generated by the AI must strictly respect these mathematical and operational constraints:

1. **Budget Integrity**:
   - Total Package Cost = Hotel Stays + Chauffeur Transit + Activities.
   - Total Cost must never exceed the traveler's stated budget ceiling (`budget_total`).
   - If traveler selects "Pay on Location", upfront checkout is reduced to essential hotel stays and chosen pre-paid items, while on-trip costs are itemized clearly for on-spot payment.

2. **Opening Hours & Chronological Sequencing**:
   - Activities must strictly fall between opening and closing hours.
   - Slot order must be strictly chronological:
     - Slot 1: Check-in or Morning stay baseline (14:00 check-in, 11:00 check-out).
     - Slot 2: Dedicated Chauffeur pickup / transit buffer (09:00 - 10:30).
     - Slot 3: Morning / Midday experience (11:00 - 13:30).
     - Slot 4: Evening highlight (16:00 - 19:30).
   - Buffer Time: A minimum transit buffer of 30 to 60 minutes must be reserved between consecutive activities.

3. **Pacing Rules**:
   - **Relaxed Pace**: 1 to 2 activities per day, late morning start (~10:30 AM), emphasis on leisure and resort amenities.
   - **Moderate Pace**: 2 to 3 activities per day with balanced midday dining and afternoon sightseeing.
   - **Packed / High-Energy Pace**: 3 to 4 activities per day, early morning start (~08:30 AM), multi-hub exploration.

4. **Weather-Aware Adaptation Rules**:
   - When rainfall exceeds **25 mm/h** or wind speeds exceed **35 km/h**:
     - Red-flag open-water activities (Scuba diving, parasailing, boat safaris).
     - Instantly propose verified sheltered alternatives (Spice plantation tours, indoor heritage galleries, culinary workshops).
   - When temperature exceeds **40°C** (e.g., Jaipur / Desert):
     - Avoid midday outdoor walking tours; shift to morning or evening slots.

## 4. Disruption Shield & Dynamic Adaptation Protocols

When an on-ground disruption occurs (bad weather, road waterlogging, hotel cancellation, ferry delay):

1. **Detection**:
   - The platform monitors Open-Meteo live API and crowdsourced traveler/lifeguard social signals.
   - If conditions exceed safety thresholds (e.g., Coast Guard hoisted red flags in Goa), the activity is marked `status: 'disrupted'`.

2. **Automated Alternative Ranking**:
   - The AI identifies the affected slot and queries inventory for verified alternatives that:
     - Match the traveler's category interests (or offer safe indoor cultural pivots).
     - Fit within the allocated time window without overlapping subsequent slots.
     - Match or decrease the allocated budget cap.
     - Have confirmed availability with contracted local suppliers.

3. **Approval Loop**:
   - The proposed alternative is transmitted to the Tour Operator Dashboard (`/operator/alerts`) and the Traveler Companion (`/trip/[id]`).
   - Upon one-click approval, the dependency graph dynamically swaps the node, recalibrates chauffeur transit buffers, and updates vendor e-vouchers.

## 5. Flexible Payment & Customization Policies

Travelers enjoy granular payment customization before checkout:
- **Necessary Core (Pre-paid Upfront)**:
  - Hotel and resort room contracts must be pre-paid upfront to lock confirmed reservation guarantees with suppliers.
- **Optional Rides & Experiences (Customizable)**:
  - Travelers can choose between:
    - *Pre-pay Now*: Bundled into upfront payment.
    - *Pay on Location / On Trip*: Reserved in schedule, but paid directly on the spot to vendor/chauffeur with ₹0 charged today.
    - *Skip*: Exclude optional items completely to lower total package valuation.
